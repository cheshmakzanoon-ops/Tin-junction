## Reference registry

Each stable ID in [registry.json](registry.json) records the original publisher, rights holder when known, exact inspected URL, access date, observed licence or absence of permission, allowed research purpose, dimensions and limitations. Unknown authorship stays unknown. A publicly viewable manufacturer page is not a production-asset licence.

- Museum collection **data** and museum **images** have different licences. The Planet entry identifies CC0 for selected metadata, CC BY 4.0 for descriptive text and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for photographs. Our record paraphrases limited facts with attribution; it does not import photographs. The noncommercial image licence is not permission for a paid app.
- The museum mechanical report is linked for technical understanding. Individual figures may have separate credits. No figure-reproduction permission is claimed.
- Manufacturer references are links and short original research notes. No permission to redistribute images, extract textures, trace designs, copy kit geometry or reuse branding has been established. Permission flags are deliberately false for this workflow.
- Those flags are project admission decisions, not a legal assertion that every possible use is forbidden. A later licence review may establish a particular use, but must record the exact work, licence, attribution, audience and derivative restrictions first.

Commercial names appear only to identify research sources. They are not proposed launch content, sponsors or product names. Runtime art remains original fictional design; no ripped game models or real operator liveries are admitted.
