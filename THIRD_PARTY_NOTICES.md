# Third-party software and asset register

This setup does not grant rights to third-party model-railway brands, train liveries, photography, music or commercial 3D assets.

## Software

The exact dependencies and transitive versions are recorded by `package-lock.json`. Before shipping, generate and review a complete license report, preserve required attribution/license text, and record the result in release evidence. Three.js is a third-party rendering library; it is not authored by this project. Development tools and their runtime implications must be reviewed separately.

## Art and sound

`assets/catalog.json` is the asset provenance authority. It begins empty. Every production asset needs a creator, source, rights record, version/hash, build provenance and review status. Do not insert dummy “approved” entries. Reference photographs are not automatically licensed for redistribution. A source URL is not a license.

## Product branding

Tin Junction remains a provisional product name pending store-name and trademark checks. Avoid reproducing NOCH, Märklin, KATO, Hornby or any other company's logos, proprietary package art or exact product trade dress. Real physical layouts are inspiration for an original miniature-world design, not a source of assets to copy.
